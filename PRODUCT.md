# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers of Dan Okhlopkov’s Russian-language Telegram channel and personal site, primarily on mobile, who follow AI tools, agent workflows, software development, and practical experiments.

## Product Purpose

Make `okhlopkov.com` the durable, shareable home for Dan’s public thinking. Long-form pages should preserve his direct personal voice while using the web for richer pacing, visual storytelling, sources, and discoverability.

## Brand Personality

Opinionated, technical, playful. The site should feel like a sharp personal artifact rather than a corporate publication or a generated content template.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/SaaS landing pages with ornamental diagrams, repeated cards, gradients, and decorative metadata.
- Over-designed magazine layouts that trade readability for art direction.
- “AI slop” copy rhythms, filler headings, and visual elements that do not add meaning.
- Desktop-first layouts that become oversized, airy, or awkward on a phone.

## Design Principles

- Mobile reading is the default; desktop should expand the composition without stretching prose.
- Let one strong image or typographic move carry a section; remove decorative UI that does not clarify the idea.
- Keep the brutalist grid visible but precise: restrained radius, hard edges, strong scale, warm dark palette.
- Preserve Dan’s spoken tempo while editing obvious repetition and typographic orphans.
- Interactive elements should behave as readers expect: whole cards are clickable, hover is subtle, focus is visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, semantic headings and links, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, descriptive image alt text, and touch targets suitable for mobile readers.
