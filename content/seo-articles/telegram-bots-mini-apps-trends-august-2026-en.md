## Telegram bot trends in August 2026: quick answer

The biggest Telegram bot trends in August 2026 were clear: gift casinos doubled, Stars resellers lost 39%, GroupHelp clones dominated the group-moderation category, and 104 doorway bots collected 20.27 million MAU by redirecting users to other bots.

> MAU means monthly active users: people who interacted with a Telegram bot during the previous month. Activity inside a Mini App does not seem to be included in this number.

Adding MAU across bots is not the same as counting unique people. One person can use several bots. Still, the public MAU number is useful for tracking direction and comparing categories over time.

## Telegram gift casinos grew 104% in one month

Sixteen gift-casino bots grew from 1.85 million to 3.78 million MAU. The category added almost two million monthly users in August.

The basic flow is simple: deposit Telegram Gifts, play a game, and try to win a more expensive gift. Telegram lets users display gifts on their profiles or convert eligible gifts into Stars; the platform documents both [Telegram Gifts](https://core.telegram.org/api/gifts) and [Telegram Stars](https://core.telegram.org/api/stars).

These products are more than simple loot boxes. They include Crash, Mines, slots, PvP games, and gift upgrades. A case is often just the front door. Gifts are treated as valuable assets: Plush Pepe was priced at 553,350 Stars during my check.

There is no blockchain deposit in this flow. You send a gift to a receiving bot, and the product turns it into a Stars balance inside the casino.

Twelve of the sixteen bots grew. This was not one lucky winner; almost the whole category moved up. The teams also appear to be separate. Each product has its own channel, support account, and deposit bot. I found only one pair that may share an owner.

The curves for [@epic_gift_bot](https://telegram.me/epic_gift_bot/app?startapp=ref_GIOhnw) and [@GorillaCaseBot](https://t.me/GorillaCaseBot/app?startapp=r_49820636) were smooth, with no daily jump above 8%. That looks like organic or viral growth rather than a large paid campaign.

[@virus_play_bot](https://t.me/virus_play_bot/app?startapp=roulette_inviteCodeWy7YVdGrIH1MaSLT) grew 5.5 times, from 240,000 to 1.32 million MAU. Its chart moved in steps, so the marketing activity is easier to see.

This is a description of the market, not a recommendation to gamble or deposit gifts into these bots.

## Telegram Stars resellers fell 39%

Telegram Stars resellers grew 4.5 times in July because several bots launched free-Star giveaways. In August, the category lost 39%. Most of the change came from only three bots.

This type of campaign attracts users quickly, but many of them do not pay. They leave as soon as the free offer ends or a paywall appears.

[@mopsstarsbot](https://t.me/mopsstarsbot) sells Telegram Stars. On July 12, it launched a giveaway and grew from 436,000 to 2.29 million MAU in one day. The campaign continued for a month, and the bot reached 3.03 million MAU.

One month later, the rolling MAU window moved past the first campaign day. On August 13, the visible number fell 58%. This suggests that most of the measured activity happened on that single day.

![MopsStarsBot MAU after a Telegram Stars giveaway: 1.5 times the baseline, but only 8% of acquired users remained](/assets/articles/telegram-bots-mini-apps-trends-august-2026/mopsstars-freebie-retention-en.webp)

Two weeks after the first spike, the chart shows slower growth, perhaps from another campaign. A similar decline starts near the end of the month.

Public data cannot tell us whether the campaign made money. At the time of the snapshot, only 8% of the acquired users remained. @starsfreerubot and @starsov ran similar campaigns in July.

This is a useful warning when reading Telegram bot trends: one promotion can move the public MAU number for an entire category.

## Doorway bots captured Telegram search traffic

While checking Telegram Stars resellers, I found a strange growth tactic. Many bots had visible MAU, but opening them produced only one message: “the bot has moved,” followed by a link to another bot.

> Example names included “STARSOVBOTT — KUPITskk ZVEZDYyc PODARKIppn” and “StarFall — Звезды Телеграм KUPITog.” They mix Latin and Cyrillic characters, place keywords in capitals, and add random letters to the end of words. Usernames are also random: @gwoqeiwqbot, @sjdlwpabot, and @ffyhddbot.

![104 Telegram doorway bots with a combined 20.3 million MAU redirect users to other stores](/assets/articles/telegram-bots-mini-apps-trends-august-2026/telegram-bot-doorways-en.webp)

I counted 104 doorway bots with more than 10,000 MAU each. Together, they had 20.27 million MAU. That is larger than the entire Telegram Stars reseller category in the market map above.

Fifty-nine of those bots used Starsov branding and had a combined 12.10 million MAU. Another ten redirected people to @mops with a message offering cheap Stars, Premium, and TON in a new bot.

It is convenient when all five bots in Telegram search for “buy Telegram Stars” redirect to your product. This is classic [search engine optimization inside Telegram](https://t.me/danokhlopkov/1678): many keyword-shaped entry points feeding one store.

This Telegram bot trend is less about product engagement and more about controlling discovery inside Telegram search.

## GroupHelp clones hold 93% of the moderation audience

![GroupHelp clones hold 93% of the measured audience for Telegram group moderation bots](/assets/articles/telegram-bots-mini-apps-trends-august-2026/grouphelp-bot-clones-en.webp)

Group moderation also has a clone pattern, partly because of Telegram Bot API limits. GroupHelp is the largest cluster. The main [@GroupHelpBot](https://t.me/GroupHelpBot) had 2 million MAU, while one official clone, [@GHClone1Bot](https://t.me/GHClone1Bot), had 2.5 million.

Few chats are likely to add several GroupHelp clones at once, but the real user count is still hard to estimate. Bots inside group chats can build large MAU numbers because messages from group members may count as interactions even when nobody mentions the bot directly.

The group-moderation category is therefore dominated by very large GroupHelp bots. The open question is whether the clones exist only because of technical limits or also work as marketing: fast growth leads to directory placement, which brings more users.

## AI wrappers, giveaway bots, and VPNs are down about 60%

![AI wrappers, giveaway bots, and VPN bots lost about 60% of MAU from their peaks](/assets/articles/telegram-bots-mini-apps-trends-august-2026/telegram-bot-categories-decline-en.webp)

General AI wrappers and giveaway bots reached their peaks in December 2025. VPN bots peaked in April 2026. By August, the three categories were down 61%, 59%, and 62% from those peaks. Blocking and regional restrictions may explain part of the decline.

One small counter-trend: classic fortune-telling bots are not growing, but bots that mention AI in their descriptions are. @arcanagurubot grew from 46,000 to 164,000 MAU.

The same split appears elsewhere in the Telegram bot market. General all-in-one AI wrappers are losing users, while focused bots for one clear use case continue to grow.

## Frequently asked questions

### What were the biggest Telegram bot trends in August 2026?

Gift casinos were the fastest-growing category, up 104% to 3.78 million MAU. Telegram Stars resellers fell 39%. GroupHelp clones controlled 93% of the measured group-moderation audience, while 104 doorway bots reached a combined 20.27 million MAU.

### How is Telegram bot MAU measured?

Telegram displays a monthly user count for bots above 10,000 MAU. The number reflects accounts that interacted with a bot during a rolling one-month window. It is useful for trend analysis, but it is not a unique-user total across the whole Telegram bot market.

### Does Telegram bot MAU include Mini App activity?

It appears to count interaction with the bot itself, not every action inside an attached Mini App. That makes MAU a useful but incomplete signal for Telegram Mini Apps trends in 2026.

### Why can total category MAU be higher than the real audience?

The same person can use several bots, and one product can operate many official clones. Group bots may also count activity from chat members. Category totals should be read as a directional market signal, not as a count of unique people.

### Does fast MAU growth mean a Telegram bot is profitable?

No. MAU shows activity, not revenue, retention, or profit. The MopsStarsBot giveaway produced a large one-day spike, but only 8% of acquired users remained in the public MAU window later.
