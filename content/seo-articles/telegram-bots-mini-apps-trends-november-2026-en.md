Between October 18 and November 17, 2026, the monthly active user counters on 20 Telegram AI assistant profiles climbed from a combined 8.20 million to 13.30 million. That was the largest measured gain across ten groups from my October report. Telegram Stars services grew fastest in percentage terms, from 3.42 million to 8.21 million MAU.

## November at a glance

- AI assistants added 5.10 million MAU. Take out the leading contributor and the remaining profiles still gained 1.51 million.
- Stars services grew 139.9%, with 38 of 58 matched profiles posting gains.
- Gifts and cases added 2.28 million MAU. Counters rose on 78 of the 145 profiles.
- Downloaders gained 1.13 million even though only 271 of 624 bots finished higher.
- The 14 bots offering AI video creation lost 27.5% of their combined MAU. Only two gained.

I share a fresh breakdown every month [on my Telegram channel](https://t.me/danokhlopkov).

## Questions about MAU

**Does the change in MAU tell me how many new users arrived?** A Telegram profile counter covers people active in the past month. It can include returning users. Some people counted in October also dropped out of the November window. The difference reflects both movements.

**Can I count Mini App users separately?** A bot that opens a Mini App still has one profile counter. It doesn’t break out chat activity and app launches. A person can also appear in several bots’ counters. My [guide to Telegram bots and Mini Apps](/en/blog/bot-revolution/) explains how the two interfaces fit together.
